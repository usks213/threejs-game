# Starting-area difficulty adjustment

PR #5 feedback: the surface starting area is too difficult before players learn the controls. This is a bounded Generator 4 encounter adjustment, not a terrain regeneration, progression reset or global damage setting.

- The surface arrival clearing around `(0, 8)` grows from 6 m to 16 m. Ordinary creatures do not target players there or enter it while pursuing someone else. Existing intruders may leave. Pending ordinary attacks cancel when all potential targets retreat; already fired ordinary projectiles are stopped at the boundary, including the player's collision radius.
- On the natural surface within 44 m of the arrival point, enemies notice players within 8 m rather than 28 m, move at 80% speed, remember targets for 2 seconds rather than 6, and do not call a starter pack. One nearby ordinary creature winds up at a time. Telegraphs last 1.25 seconds and recovery between attacks lasts 4 seconds. Cinderunners use close attacks instead of charging. Ordinary hits deal at most 3 physical damage without poison, frost or burn.
- New worlds have two separated, 24-health slimes just outside the clearing. Both fall to two normal hits with the starting club. The other three authored enemies beyond the beginner region retain their positions, identities, health and roles. Procedural exploration does not add further enemies within the starter surface region.
- Existing Generator 4 saves retain every enemy, its health, identity, stars, loot/respawn state, and world progression. They receive the same local behavior tuning immediately. No saved actors or terrain edits are deleted and no schema/protocol migration is needed. Already visited tiles are not reseeded.
- The local tuning ends beyond the 44 m region and does not cover the underground or sky routes. Bosses, other generators, unrelated damage, resources, terrain, water and save format remain unchanged. Multiplayer uses each actual target's position, so a player sheltering in the clearing does not pause another player's combat.

Verification: `beginner-encounters.test.ts` covers new spawn density and persisted tile revisit, detection, no recruitment, serialized ranged/close attacks, damage/status effects, retreat, another participant outside the clearing, hostile projectile sweeps and edge contacts, normal later/sky/cave behavior, and old-save identity preservation. `entry-clearing.test.ts` uses the actual seeded SessionAuthority for arrival, gathering, movement and the first live enemy encounter. Existing encounter, pack, save, spawn and navigation suites are also run. These are deterministic authority/unit checks; actual phone balance review remains separate.
