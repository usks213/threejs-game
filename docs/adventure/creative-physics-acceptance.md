## Element priority and bounded work

1. Authored anchored trial/site slabs retain integrity and never burn/freeze from these reactions.
2. Wetness extinguishes and takes priority over fire/explosion. Fire first spends heat drying wet material.
3. Frozen parts first thaw under fire; they do not ignite or explode in the same reaction.
4. Dry charged batteries with at least 10 energy release that stored energy once as a radius-3 fire pulse, at most 24 enemy damage; energy becomes zero. The battery remains as a physical part unless ordinary integrity damage eventually destroys it. Player friendly-fire remains disabled.
5. Dry wood ignites. Shock propagates only through linked metal/wet/battery conductors. Wind can spread existing fire at most 2m downwind, subject to voxel/rock occlusion.
6. One reaction visits at most 8 parts; at most 32 gameplay effects are issued per tick across repeated calls. Periodic flame spreading starts at most 16 new fires per tick. Hitting a budget stops further propagation; it does not delete unaffected objects.
7. Recall history contains no charge, integrity, wetness, burning, frost, inventory or progression. Destruction retains loaded cargo as its same owner-protected physical wreck.

## Contact presentation and replay semantics

Contact/explosion presentation events are authority-issued and snapshot-only. Each event ID includes a per-`SkyboundPowers` random runtime epoch, authority tick and increasing event sequence. The epoch is only a presentation identifier and does not affect physics decisions. Reconstructing from a save creates a new epoch, even at the same tick/part ID; persistent saves contain no old events.

At most 16 events per tick and 64 recent events are retained, with a 30-tick (one-second) retention window. Repeated collisions from a part have a 12-tick cooldown and normal impacts below strength 1 are silent. Audio and particle consumers each keep a separate 256-ID cursor. Repeated/full-resync snapshots do not replay an already consumed ID. A new view or full page reload may play still-recent events once, because it has a new presentation cursor; old events beyond the window are ignored. This is best-effort sensory presentation, not a durable exactly-once gameplay queue.

## Explicit limits

No game mechanics, inventory identities, boat hull geometry, water volume preservation or camp storage are removed. Exact fluid pressure coupling, independent suspension, a commercial continuous rigid-body solver, arbitrary large-world replay, live-device FPS and public multiplayer acceptance remain outside this local result or require separate measurement. The original scope's physical behavior is implemented with the disclosed bounded game models. Final combined typecheck/unit/build and public verification belong to the integration gate.

## Crouch persistence and prediction details

`SavedPlayer.crouching` is an optional boolean, with absent values retaining original standing dimensions. It is emitted for a crouched root player and preserved in participant exports. Validation rejects non-boolean values. No save version or geometry generator changes. This is needed when the requested sneak flag is off but a ceiling prevents the actual body from expanding.

Actor switching explicitly projects and restores false values, so a guest's optional posture cannot leak into the host through `Object.assign`. Peer appearance writes the correct actor's actual posture after transient traversal fields. Client reconciliation treats a missing posture field as standing. Prediction calls traversal with `authoritative=false`, so submerged breath simulation cannot apply client-side health damage.

Local evidence: the six dedicated crouch cases cover low terrain passage, blocked stand/feet preservation, building/part/rock headroom, Guest/Host posture separation, avatar height/restoration, save roundtrip and malformed posture rejection. Root TypeScript and the Cloudflare coop TypeScript project pass. Final combined checks and public acceptance are tracked separately.
