# ADR 0015: Original riding and towing companion

Status: implemented locally; public preview and device validation pending.

The Generator 4 world adds three original lantern-backed **Lumer (灯背獣ルメル)** creatures. This does not replace or reduce existing wildlife or the unrestricted rock collection. The independent version-1 companion save is capped at eight creatures. Each creature records pose, stamina, bonding, owner, following mode and optional tow attachment. Rider and movement controls are transient and are never resumed from disk.

Three individual berry transactions establish a bond. The first feeding reserves ownership, so another participant cannot finish or steal an in-progress bond. Only the owner can call, ride or attach a rope. Authority checks 3D range and line of sight. Riding occupies one creature and one player; vehicle, climbing and gliding mode changes require dismount. Authority input expiry stops a disconnected or silent rider. Leaving, becoming downed or returning home releases riding, following and the rope. Rejoining retains the bond, not a stale seat claim.

Movement is a bounded upright sampled body with two movement substeps. It checks terrain, carved building/tree geometry, SkyPart shapes, rocks, other creatures and players, plus headroom for the rider. Surface lookup stays in the current layer. A creature avoids steep steps, large ledges and deep water; terrain removed below it produces vertical falling. Dismount searches checked nearby landing points and the last boarding position. Forced dismount can use checked air above a spawn location when normal exits are blocked. It does not guarantee a landing surface in an adversarial imported world containing no free space.

A rope accepts up to eight connected non-fixed, non-trial parts with total mass at most 60, owned by the caller or explicitly shared. Towing, grab, replay and seat ownership are mutually exclusive. A slack length of 3 m, force cap of 90 and break distance of 7 m bound the impulse applied through the existing Skybound rigid-body API. A newly blocking wall, changed graph, excessive weight, missing part or missing owner releases the rope. Loading revalidates a saved attachment; it never trusts a saved lock.

This is deliberately an arcade walking body and a bounded spring rope, not a deformable animal or a two-way mass-conserving animal/vehicle constraint solver. Lumer currently has no combat/death model, breeding, swimming, animation-state locomotion solver or navigation around blocked paths. Follow stops at obstacles instead of teleporting or pathfinding. Remote input prediction holds the authoritative riding position rather than independently simulating a second creature.

Evidence: `tests/unit/companions.test.ts` covers food cost, ownership conflict, mounted motion and stamina, walls/floors, following, disconnect/rejoin, actual assembly displacement, grab/replay/seat exclusion, saved bond/rope recovery, new rope occlusion and graph mass limits, stale input cancellation, real terrain seed positions, and eight-creature fixed probe budget. Rendering, browser controls and public transport verification are separate integration gates.
